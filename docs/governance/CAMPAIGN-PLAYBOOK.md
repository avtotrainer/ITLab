# Organic and Paid Campaign Playbook
Version 1.0 | 2026-10-23 | State: Planning only

## Campaign specification
Each campaign must have 1) specific objective; 2) segment and exclusions; 3) platform and rationale; 4) core claim/offer; 5) asset format and rights; 6) organic/paid strategy; 7) budget and duration when paid; 8) clear call to action; 9) verified landing destination; 10) KPIs and attribution; 11) privacy, consent and platform rules; 12) evaluation, stop criteria and next iteration.

## Organic example — troubleshooting knowledge
Objective: get qualified IT practitioners to use a tested printer handout. Audience: Georgian technical educators, NixOS users and Linux printer administrators. Channel: LinkedIn technical post and optional X thread. Offer: actionable troubleshooting method with evidence and verification. Creative: concise before/after problem description without disclosing private topology. CTA: read canonical GitHub handout. Measure: engaged questions and useful feedback, not fabricated impressions. Risks: version specificity and sensitive screenshots.

## Paid experiment template
Only after approved account, destination, current policy and explicit budget: choose one audience, one message and one measurable action; use a bounded total cap and duration approved by the user. Verify platform eligibility, campaign objective/targeting, creative, payment settings and tracking consent before launch. Record actual cost and results after completion. Do not invent a recommended numeric budget or benchmark in the absence of requirements.

## Review gates
Before paid activation, review official live policies, currency/location eligibility, privacy, student-related content, prohibited/restricted targeting and approvals. References: https://business.x.com/en/help/ads-policies ; https://business.x.com/en/advertising/get-started-with-twitter-ads ; https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a424397/linkedin-company-and-contact-targeting-overview?lang=en-US
Do not upload contact lists without consent. Never launch paid advertising, spend or change billing without separate explicit authorization.

## Outcome log
Campaign ID, approved owner, goals, content links, dates, approved cap, actual spend if any, actual KPIs, decision, links to evidence. Unlaunched ideas are `proposed`, not `successful`.
