## Synchronization interpretation
- `in-sync` means the canonical content and Google Doc were compared after whitespace normalization and Markdown-heading conversion on 2026-10-23; Docs preserves headings as named styles. It does **not** assert byte-for-byte Markdown identity.
- `canonical-only` means no verified Google Docs mirror exists.
- `unknown` means the relationship/content parity was not checked.
- `docs-ahead`, `repo-ahead`, and `conflict` are reserved for later revision comparisons.
- Existing HP printer article and its Google Doc were discovered but **not** compared for substantive parity.
- Dates marked editorial only are not evidence of technical procedure execution.
- The two governance Docs are private by current returned metadata; they are not publicly readable by default.

## Operational protocol
Before replacing a mirror, read both current revisions, compare independently made edits and record direction. Never overwrite collaborator contributions automatically. After changes, read back location and content, update this register, and link related documents. Avoid automatically translating Georgian originals.
